Oorbit A personal desktop project
Games, films, series & more

Everything you follow.
One place.

Orbit is a non-commercial Windows desktop application that brings your entertainment activity together: video games and playtime, films and television series, ratings, and watch progress. More sections are planned for the future. Your personal collection stays on your computer.

Your collection

Keep your games, films, and series together in one personal library, with playtime, ratings, and titles you want to discover.

Series progress

Track a series as you watch, including your current season and episode. New sections can be added as Orbit grows.

Artwork lookup

Orbit can search TMDB for poster artwork using a read-only API key entered by the app owner.

This product uses the TMDB API but is not endorsed or certified by TMDB.The Movie Database (TMDB)
